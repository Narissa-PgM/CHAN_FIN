    The Dean’s AAA framework

    Agile

    A monthly forecast and a quarterly scenario refresh, so decisions move with the federal picture, not a year behind it.

    Accessible

    Every package ends in a one-page brief that a non-financial leader can act on, with the same numbers as the detail.

    Accountable

    Every variance over $250K has a named owner and a driver. The downside scenario is not a prediction but a set of agreed triggers, so levers are pulled before a deficit grows.

    Where FP&A supports the strategy

    A smaller, focused School

    A margin view of every center and program, so the portfolio review is based on full costs, including space and administration.

    Convergence science

    Cost-sharing rules for cross-department work, agreed once, so joint projects are not slowed by budget questions.

    Education innovation

    Business cases and monthly margin tracking for the Advanced Learning Academy and the online SM.

    Industry partnerships

    Pricing that recovers full indirect costs on corporate research, checked before each agreement is signed.

    Rebuilding the reserve

    A reserve target in the multi-year plan, so recovery years rebuild resilience instead of restoring every past cost.

    One Harvard finance

    The monthly meeting of FP&A directors across the schools, to share assumptions on federal funding and the endowment tax.

    To the Dean · From Financial Planning and Analysis · November 2026 · FY27 forecast after the October close

    We expect to finish the year $4M short, $3M better than budget. The pressure on our own funds is greater than that number shows.

    $403Mrevenue, $7M below budget
    $407Mspending, $10M below budget
    $4.5Mlost from indirect costs and tuition, with no matching saving

    What changed this month

    • New federal awards are still about a quarter below their usual pace. Most of that shortfall is matched by lower spending on those awards.
    • The indirect costs those awards would have paid, and lower international enrollment, are the real pressure on unrestricted funds.
    • Executive and online education and corporate research are ahead of plan, by $3.5M together.

    Decisions we need from you

    1. Keep the 22 vacant positions on hold through January.
    2. Approve full indirect-cost pricing as the standard for all new corporate research agreements.
    3. Ask departments for FY28 plans against the base scenario, with a downside version prepared.

    Risks we are watching

    The First Circuit ruling, FY27 federal appropriations, any new visa restriction, and how the endowment tax is shared among schools. None is in the forecast yet. Each has a scenario ready.
