    R / Readjust

    What changes when a trigger fires?

    Evaluation identifies the deviation. Readjustment changes the plan, records the decision and resets the next cycle.

    01 · Detect

    Watch funding, forecast error, restriction exceptions and delivery dates.

    02 · Test

    Refresh scenarios; compare feasible responses and mission effects.

    03 · Approve

    Document the recommendation, authorized decision, owner and effective date.

    04 · Reset

    Version the forecast, update commitments and communicate the change.
